Everything it does

One app for watching, fixing and making videos.

Play

Almost anything, in the browser

  • ▶️

    Plays almost anything

    Local video and audio, HLS streams, IPTV lists, YouTube links and live streams, including AC-3, DTS, TrueHD and ProRes.

  • ⏪

    Play it backwards

    A playback direction, with sound, at any speed.

  • ⏩

    Speed and A–B loop

    0.25× to 2×, and loop any stretch.

  • 💬

    Subtitles

    Load .srt or .vtt files, or generate them on your device with AI (Whisper).

  • 🎚️

    Sound

    Equaliser (bass, mid, treble), and choice of audio track.

  • 🎨

    Filters and fun FX

    Presets, live effects and stickers while it plays.

  • 📸

    Screenshots

    Save the frame on screen.

  • 📂

    Playlists and folders

    Add files, whole folders or links; search the list.

Your library

Your files, wherever you are

  • 🖥️

    Stream your own library

    The desktop app shares a folder; open it on your phone or laptop with a link.

  • ⚡

    Smart buffering

    Downloads ahead of what you watch; the seek bar shows what is ready, even on a slow link.

  • 📴

    Works offline

    Install it as an app; after a first visit it opens and plays local files with no network.

Edit

In a side panel, with the player as the preview

  • ✂️

    Cut and multi-cut

    Keep one part, or many.

  • 🔲

    Crop

    Drag the corners on the picture.

  • 🔄

    Rotate and resize

    Turn it, or change its size.

  • 🐢

    Speed and reverse

    Save it faster, slower or backwards.

  • 💧

    Blur and watermark

    Hide a part, or add your mark.

  • 🎭

    Remove background

    On your device, no green screen.

  • ✨

    Export with effects

    Save it with the filters and stickers you see.

  • ↩️

    Undo and reset

    Edits stack on one copy, and are remembered after a reload.

Save as

The format you need

  • 🔁

    Convert and compress

    Another format, or a smaller file.

  • 🎞️

    GIF

    Any stretch, as an animated GIF.

  • 🎼

    Tracks

    Take out the audio, video or subtitle tracks.

  • 🔐

    Encrypt

    Hide a video inside an ordinary-looking one that survives re-uploading.

Analyse

Find what matters

  • 🎬

    Scene detection

    Finds the cuts, to jump between or export.

  • 🏃

    Motion detection

    Finds where something moves.

  • ℹ️

    Info

    Codecs, tracks, sizes and more.

Create and share

Make something new

  • 🔴

    Record your screen

    Or your camera.

  • ➕

    Merge videos

    Several clips, one video.

  • 🖼️

    Images to video

    A slideshow with transitions and music.

  • 🎵

    Combine audio and video

    The picture of one, the sound of another.

  • 👥

    Watch together

    Watch with friends, peer to peer.

Private by design

Your videos stay yours

  • 🔒

    Nothing is uploaded

    Files are played and edited on your device.

  • ⚙️

    Long jobs in the background

    Conversions and exports run one at a time while you keep watching.

  • 💸

    Free

    No account, no sign-up.
