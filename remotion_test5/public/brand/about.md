# Brand Brief: JellyJump Player

## Company Overview
**Name:** JellyJump Player
**Tagline:** PLAY. EDIT. CREATE. YOUR VIDEOS.
**Mission:** To provide a powerful, privacy-first, client-side video player and editing suite for everyone.

## Product/Service
**Core Offering:** A web-based video player and editor.
**Key Features:**
- **Local Processing:** 100% Client-side. No server uploads.
- **Tools:** Trim, Convert, Resize, Merge, GIF creation, Recording.
- **Playback:** HLS support, A-B Loop, Speed Control, Frame Stepping.
- **Engine:** Powered by MediaBunny (WebCodecs).

## Target Audience
- **Demographics:** Video editors, content creators, developers, privacy-conscious users.
- **Psychographics:** Values privacy, speed, open-source, and browser-based tools.

## Visual Identity
- **Style:** Cyberpunk, Retro-Futuristic, Minimalist, Tech-focused.
- **Colors:** 
    - Primary: Neon Green (#00ff88)
    - Background: Dark (#0a0a0a or #2a2a2a)
    - Text: Silver/Grey (#b0b0b0)
- **Fonts:** Space Grotesk, Space Mono (Monospace styling).

## Call to Action
- "LAUNCH PLAYER"
- "Try Player"
- "Download .deb / .dmg"
