# Voice & Tone Guidelines: JellyJump Player

## Brand Personality
- **Primary:** Tech-Savvy, Direct, Private.
- **Secondary:** Innovative, Efficient, Open.

## Tone of Voice
- **Direct:** "Brutally simple."
- **Empowering:** "Powerful video tools."
- **Technical:** Focus on "Client-side", "WebCodecs", "Local".

## Key Terminology
- **Do Use:** "Client-side", "Privacy", "Local", "WebCodecs", "MediaBunny", "HLS", "Timeline".
- **Avoid:** "Cloud", "Upload", "Server", "Subscription".

## Script Guidelines (Ad Copy)

### Hook
Focus on the privacy or the power of the browser.
*Example:* "Video editing without the upload."
*Example:* "Your browser is more powerful than you think."

### Problem/Agitation
Slow uploads, privacy concerns, heavy software.
*Example:* "Tired of waiting for uploads?"
*Example:* "Don't trust the cloud with your footage?"

### Solution
JellyJump's local processing.
*Example:* "JellyJump Player: 100% Client-side editing."
*Example:* "Edit, convert, and play instantly."

### CTA
Launch or Download.
*Example:* "Launch Player Now."
