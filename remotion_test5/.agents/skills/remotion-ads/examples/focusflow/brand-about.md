## Company Overview

- **Company Name:** FocusFlow
- **Tagline:** "Deep work, made simple."
- **Industry:** Productivity / Software
- **Website:** focusflow.app
- **Founded:** 2024

### Mission Statement
Help busy professionals reclaim their focus and achieve deep work in a world full of distractions.

### Vision
Become the go-to productivity companion for 10 million knowledge workers by 2027.

---

## Product/Service

### Primary Offering
FocusFlow is a minimalist focus timer and distraction blocker app that helps users achieve deep work sessions through science-backed techniques.

### Key Features
1. Smart Pomodoro timer with AI-adjusted intervals
2. Website and app blocker during focus sessions
3. Focus music and ambient sounds library
4. Daily/weekly focus analytics and streaks
5. Team focus sessions for remote teams

### Unique Selling Proposition (USP)
Unlike complex productivity suites, FocusFlow does one thing exceptionally well: helps you focus. No bloat, no learning curve, just deep work.

### Pain Points Solved
1. Constant social media and notification distractions
2. Difficulty starting and maintaining focus sessions
3. No visibility into actual productive hours
4. Feeling overwhelmed by complex productivity tools

### Price Point
Mid-range ($9.99/month or $79/year, 7-day free trial)

## Target Audience

### Primary Demographics
- **Age Range:** 25-45
- **Gender:** All
- **Location:** US, UK, Canada, Australia (English-speaking)
- **Income Level:** Middle to upper-middle ($50k-150k)
- **Occupation:** Knowledge workers, developers, designers, writers, students

### Psychographics
- **Interests:** Self-improvement, productivity, minimalism, tech
- **Values:** Efficiency, work-life balance, personal growth
- **Lifestyle:** Busy professionals, remote workers, side-hustlers
- **Pain Points:** Distracted, overwhelmed, struggling to do deep work

### Customer Avatar
Sarah is a 32-year-old product manager at a tech startup. She works remotely and struggles with constant Slack notifications and the temptation to check social media. She's tried multiple productivity apps but found them too complex. She wants something simple that just works.

## Brand Personality

### Brand Archetype
Sage (wise, knowledgeable, helps others achieve clarity)

### Personality Traits
- Calm
- Focused
- Supportive
- Minimalist
- Trustworthy

### If Your Brand Were a Person
FocusFlow would be a calm, knowledgeable friend who doesn't lecture you about productivity but gently guides you toward better habits. They speak simply and clearly, wear clean minimalist clothing, and always seem centered and present.

## Content Restrictions

### Must Include
- Brand name "FocusFlow" in every ad
- 7-day free trial mention
- "Link in Bio" for Instagram

### Must Avoid
- Claims about curing ADHD or medical conditions
- Comparisons that name competitors directly
- Overly aggressive sales language

### Legal/Compliance Notes
- Include "Results may vary" for productivity claims
- No medical/health claims
