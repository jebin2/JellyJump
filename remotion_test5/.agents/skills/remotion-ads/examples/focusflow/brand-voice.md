# Voice & Messaging Guidelines - FocusFlow

> Example voice guidelines for the remotion-ads skill demo.
> Copy this structure to `public/brand/voice-guidelines.md` for your own brand.

---

## Tone of Voice

### Primary Tone
Calm + Supportive (like a wise friend, not a drill sergeant)

### Tone by Context

| Context | Tone | Example |
|---------|------|---------|
| Hook/Attention | Empathetic, relatable | "Sound familiar?" |
| Problem Statement | Understanding, validating | "It's not your fault..." |
| Solution/Benefits | Confident, reassuring | "There's a better way." |
| Call-to-Action | Inviting, low-pressure | "Try it free for 7 days." |
| Testimonials | Authentic, conversational | "I finally feel in control." |

## Words to Avoid

### Overused/Cliché
- Hustle
- Grind
- Crush it
- 10x
- Hack
- Game-changer

### Off-Brand
- Aggressive language
- Complicated jargon
- Hyperbolic claims
- FOMO-inducing urgency

### Legally Sensitive
- "Cure" or "treat" (re: ADHD)
- "Guaranteed results"
- "Best app ever"
- Specific competitor names

### Competitor Terms
- Forest (trees, planting)
- Pomodoro (trademarked in some contexts)

## Punctuation & Style

### Emoji Usage
Sparingly (1-2 per post max, focus-related only: 🎯✨🧠)

### Hashtag Style
3-5 relevant: #productivity #focusmode #deepwork #remotework #focusflow

### Capitalization
Sentence case for body, Title Case for headlines

### Numbers
Use digits (7 days, 3 hours, 50,000 users)

## Script Templates

### 15-Second Ad Script Structure
```
HOOK (2-3s): "Can't focus? You're not alone."

PROBLEM (3-4s): "Notifications. Social media. The endless scroll."

SOLUTION (4-5s): "FocusFlow blocks distractions so you can do deep work."

CTA (2-3s): "Try free for 7 days. Link in bio."
```

### 30-Second Ad Script Structure
```
HOOK (3-4s): "What if you could add 3 hours of real work to your day?"

PROBLEM (5-7s): "We're drowning in distractions. Notifications every minute.
                The urge to check social media. Hours disappear into the void."

SOLUTION (8-10s): "FocusFlow is your focus companion. One tap to block
                  distractions. Smart timers that adapt to you.
                  Finally, deep work made simple."

SOCIAL PROOF (4-5s): "Join 50,000 professionals who've reclaimed their focus."

CTA (3-4s): "Start your free trial today. Link in bio."
```

## Voiceover Preferences

### Preferred Voice Type
Male or Female (warm, calm voice)

### Voice Characteristics
Calm, Warm, Reassuring, Confident (not aggressive)

### Speaking Pace
Medium (2.8 words per second)

### Character Presets to Use
- Primary: `narrator`
- For Hooks: `dramatic`
- For CTAs: `calm`

## Sample Scripts

### Good Example
```
[HOOK - dramatic]
"Three hours. That's how much focus time the average person loses to distractions every single day."

[PROBLEM - narrator]
"Notifications pulling you away. Social media calling your name. That project you meant to finish... still waiting."

[SOLUTION - expert]
"FocusFlow changes everything. One tap activates focus mode. Distractions blocked. Your best work, unlocked."

[CTA - calm]
"Try it free for 7 days. Deep work, made simple. Link in bio."
```

### What to Avoid
```
❌ "STOP BEING LAZY! This app will 10x your productivity GUARANTEED!
    Download NOW before it's too late! Only WINNERS use FocusFlow!
    CRUSH your goals! HUSTLE HARDER!"

(Too aggressive, makes unverifiable claims, uses off-brand language)
```

## Localization Notes

### Primary Language
English (US)

### Regional Variations
- US: "Try free" (not "Try for free")
- UK: Consider "focussed" spelling in UK-targeted ads

### Translation Considerations
- "Deep work" concept may need explanation in some markets
- "Link in bio" is Instagram-specific, adjust for other platforms
